Vesta Privacy Terms

Vesta

A household assistant for one family, their home, and the person who helps them run it.

Vesta keeps track of a single household. She reads the family's calendars, answers questions about who is free and when, remembers what she has been told, and replies in a private Slack workspace shared by three people. She is named for the hearth — the fire kept alive so the house stays warm, tended so that nobody else has to think about it.

She is not a product and is not for sale. There is no sign-up, no account to create, and no version of this that runs for anyone else. She exists because a household wanted one, and she runs on a computer in that household.

What she does

  • Reads the family's Google Calendars and reports what is on them.
  • Finds open time across several calendars at once, so a plan can be made in one question.
  • Creates and updates events when asked, and answers invitations.
  • Keeps short written notes of things she has been told, in a plain text file the family can read and edit.
  • Answers in the language she is spoken to, in a Slack workspace with three members.

Where she runs

On one personal computer. There is no server, no hosted service, and no database anywhere else. Calendar data is fetched when a question is asked and used to write the answer. Access tokens are stored in a single file on that machine, readable only by its owner.

Nothing about this household is sent to an analytics service, an advertiser, or any third party. There is nobody to send it to.

Who uses her

Three named people: the two members of the household and one person hired to help at home. No one else can reach her, and no one else's data passes through her.

Google account access

Vesta requests one Google scope, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar, so she can read and manage the calendars belonging to the accounts that authorised her. The privacy policy sets out exactly what is accessed, what it is used for, where it is kept, and how to revoke it. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
